# Extra Documentation

Special thanks for collectors and conservators that preserved not only 40yrs+ of technology but also documentation in form of manuals, technical datasheets and even catalogue material!

## Official Manuals
 * [Bedienungsanleitung](http://www.mpm-kc85.de/dokupack/ErikaS3004_bda.pdf) Scan of the user manual. For basic operation and maintance. Enjoy GDR aesthetics of the 1980s. :-)
 * [Serviceanleitung](https://www.manualslib.de/manual/1097773/Robotron-Erika-3004-Electronic.html) Scan of the technical support manual- Very useful, lot of schematics for the electronics and mechanical parts.

## About Erika Typewriters in General
 * [http://erika-electronic.de/](http://erika-electronic.de/) Fanpage of a collector with lots of original scans of documents and photos of different variants.
 * [https://typewriterdatabase.com/1987-erika-3004-electronic.11746.typewriter](https://typewriterdatabase.com/1987-erika-3004-electronic.11746.typewriter) Erika S 3004 in the typewriter database. Includes also printed typesets.
